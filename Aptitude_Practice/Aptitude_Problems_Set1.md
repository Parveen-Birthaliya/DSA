# Aptitude Practice - Time & Work, Profit & Loss, Percentages

## 📚 Section 1: Percentages & Ratios

### **Key Formulas:**

1. **Percentage = (Value/Total) × 100**
2. **Increase% = [(New - Old)/Old] × 100**
3. **Decrease% = [(Old - New)/Old] × 100**
4. **Successive Change:** 
   - If +a% then +b%, net = a + b + (ab/100)
5. **Ratio to Percentage:** 
   - If A:B = 3:5, then A = 3/8 × 100 = 37.5%

---

### **Practice Problems (30 Questions):**

#### Level 1: Easy (1-10)

**1.** What is 25% of 200?
- A) 40  B) 50  C) 60  D) 75
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 50 | Solution: (25/100) × 200 = 50</details>

**2.** If a number is increased by 20%, what is the multiplier?
- A) 1.2  B) 0.8  C) 1.02  D) 2.0
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 1.2 | Solution: 100% + 20% = 120% = 1.2</details>

**3.** 60 is what percent of 300?
- A) 15%  B) 20%  C) 25%  D) 30%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 20% | Solution: (60/300) × 100 = 20%</details>

**4.** A:B = 2:3 and B:C = 4:5. Find A:B:C.
- A) 2:3:5  B) 8:12:15  C) 2:4:5  D) 4:6:15
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 8:12:15 | Solution: Make B same: A:B = 8:12, B:C = 12:15</details>

**5.** If price increases by 25%, by what % should consumption decrease to keep expense same?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 33.33%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 20% | Solution: x/(100+x) × 100 = 25/125 × 100 = 20%</details>

**6.** Find 15% of 60 + 25% of 80.
- A) 29  B) 30  C) 31  D) 32
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 29 | Solution: 9 + 20 = 29</details>

**7.** If A is 40% more than B, then B is what % less than A?
- A) 28.57%  B) 40%  C) 30%  D) 35%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 28.57% | Solution: 40/140 × 100 = 28.57%</details>

**8.** Two numbers are in ratio 3:4. If each is increased by 6, ratio becomes 4:5. Find the numbers.
- A) 15, 20  B) 18, 24  C) 12, 16  D) 21, 28
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 18, 24 | Solution: (3x+6)/(4x+6) = 4/5 → x = 6</details>

**9.** A number is increased by 10% and then decreased by 10%. Net change?
- A) 0%  B) 1% decrease  C) 1% increase  D) 2% decrease
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 1% decrease | Solution: 10 - 10 - (10×10/100) = -1%</details>

**10.** If 30% of a number is 90, what is the number?
- A) 270  B) 300  C) 330  D) 360
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 300 | Solution: x × 0.3 = 90 → x = 300</details>

## 📚 Section 2: Time & Work

### **Key Formulas:**

1. **Work = Time × Efficiency**
2. **Total Work = LCM(days)**
3. **One day work = 1/Total days**
4. **Combined efficiency = Sum of individual efficiencies**
5. **If A can do work in 'a' days, one day work = 1/a**

### **Important Concepts:**
- If A is twice as good as B, then A:B efficiency = 2:1
- If A+B complete in x days, B+C in y days, C+A in z days
  - 2(A+B+C) = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z

---

### **Practice Problems (30 Questions):**

#### Level 1: Easy (1-10)

**1.** A can do work in 10 days. In how many days can 5 men (with same efficiency as A) complete it?
- A) 2  B) 3  C) 5  D) 4
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 2 | Solution: 10/5 = 2 days</details>

**2.** A can do work in 15 days, B in 20 days. Together in how many days?
- A) 8.57 days  B) 10 days  C) 12 days  D) 7 days
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 8.57 days | Solution: 1/15 + 1/20 = 7/60 → 60/7 days</details>

**3.** A completes work in 12 days. He works 4 days then B joins. Together they finish in 3 more days. B's time?
- A) 9 days  B) 12 days  C) 15 days  D) 18 days
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 9 days | Solution: Work left = 2/3, Done by A+B in 3 days → B = 9 days</details>

**4.** 10 men complete work in 15 days. In how many days will 25 men complete?
- A) 6  B) 8  C) 10  D) 5
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 6 | Solution: (10×15)/25 = 6 days</details>

**5.** A is twice as efficient as B. A takes 20 days. B takes?
- A) 40 days  B) 10 days  C) 30 days  D) 50 days
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 40 days | Solution: If A:B = 2:1, time ratio = 1:2</details>

**6.** A can do in 10 days, B in 15 days. They work together for 4 days. Remaining work?
- A) 1/3  B) 2/3  C) 1/6  D) 5/6
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 1/3 | Solution: 4(1/10 + 1/15) = 4(1/6) = 2/3 done, 1/3 left</details>

**7.** 12 workers complete work in 8 days. After 3 days, 4 workers leave. Total days to complete?
- A) 12  B) 11  C) 10  D) 13
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 11 | Solution: 3 days + (5/8)/(8/96) = 3 + 7.5 = 10.5 ≈ 11</details>

**8.** A does 2/5 of work in 10 days. Days to complete full work?
- A) 20  B) 25  C) 30  D) 15
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 25 | Solution: (10×5)/2 = 25 days</details>

**9.** A and B together in 6 days, B and C in 8 days. A and C together?
(Given A alone takes 12 days)
- A) 8 days  B) 10 days  C) 9.6 days  D) 12 days
<details><summary>Answer</summary>C) 9.6 days | Solution: Use efficiency formula</details>

**10.** Pipe A fills tank in 20 min, B empties in 30 min. Together in how much time to fill?
- A) 60 min  B) 50 min  C) 40 min  D) 45 min
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 60 min | Solution: 1/20 - 1/30 = 1/60</details>

## 📚 Section 3: Profit & Loss

### **Key Formulas:**

1. **Profit = SP - CP**
2. **Loss = CP - SP**
3. **Profit% = (Profit/CP) × 100**
4. **Loss% = (Loss/CP) × 100**
5. **SP = CP(1 + Profit%/100)** or **CP(1 - Loss%/100)**
6. **CP = SP/(1 + Profit%/100)** or **SP/(1 - Loss%/100)**
7. **Discount% = (Discount/MP) × 100**
8. **SP = MP(1 - Discount%/100)**

---

### **Practice Problems (30 Questions):**

#### Level 1: Easy (1-10)

**1.** CP = 500, SP = 600. Profit%?
- A) 20%  B) 15%  C) 25%  D) 10%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 20% | Solution: (100/500) × 100 = 20%</details>

**2.** Marked price is 20% above CP. Discount is 10%. Profit%?
- A) 8%  B) 10%  C) 12%  D) 15%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 8% | Solution: SP = 120 × 0.9 = 108, Profit = 8%</details>

**3.** Loss of 10% on selling for 540. CP?
- A) 600  B) 550  C) 580  D) 620
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 600 | Solution: CP = 540/0.9 = 600</details>

**4.** Two articles sold at 990 each. One at 10% profit, other at 10% loss. Net?
- A) No profit no loss  B) 1% loss  C) 1% profit  D) 2% loss
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 1% loss | Solution: Always loss in this case = (loss%/10)² = 1%</details>

**5.** If CP doubled, profit% becomes?
- A) Halved  B) Doubled  C) Same  D) One-fourth
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) Halved | Solution: Profit amount same, but CP doubled</details>

**6.** MP = 1200, SP = 960. Discount%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 15%  D) 30%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 20% | Solution: (240/1200) × 100 = 20%</details>

**7.** Profit of 25% on CP. If CP is 800, find SP.
- A) 900  B) 1000  C) 1100  D) 950
<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) 1000 | Solution: 800 × 1.25 = 1000</details>

**8.** A shopkeeper marks 30% above CP and gives 10% discount. Profit%?
- A) 17%  B) 18%  C) 20%  D) 15%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 17% | Solution: 130 × 0.9 = 117, Profit = 17%</details>

**9.** CP of 12 oranges = SP of 15 oranges. Loss%?
- A) 20%  B) 25%  C) 30%  D) 15%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 20% | Solution: CP/SP = 12/15 = 4/5, Loss = 20%</details>

**10.** Successive discounts of 20% and 10%. Single equivalent discount?
- A) 28%  B) 30%  C) 32%  D) 25%
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A) 28% | Solution: 20 + 10 - (20×10/100) = 28%</details>
