**Project Type:** Major Project | **Duration:** 4 Months | **Team:** 4 Members

**Problem Statement:**  
Growing waste disposal crisis and need for clean energy inspired this project to convert waste into electricity while controlling pollution.

**Solution Implemented:**
- Designed combustion chamber for controlled burning of biodegradable waste materials
- Implemented heating process to convert waste into high-temperature thermal energy

**Technical Specifications:**
- Operating Temperature: 150-250°C
- Power Output: 10-15 Watts

- Pollution Reduction: ~60% emission reduction with carbon filter

### **2️⃣ ELECTRICITY GENERATION USING SPEED BREAKER**
**Project Type:** Minor Project | **Duration:** 3 Months | **Team:** 4 Members

**Problem Statement:**  
Kinetic energy from vehicles passing over speed breakers goes to waste. This project captures this energy to generate electricity.

**Solution Implemented:**
- Designed and installed rack and pinion gear mechanism beneath speed breaker
- Vertical pressure from vehicles converted to rotary motion through gearing system
- Rotary shaft connected to DC generator (12V, 5W) for electricity generation
- Integrated spring return system for automatic mechanism reset
- Battery storage system (12V, 7Ah) for energy accumulation
- Voltage regulator circuit for stable output voltage

**Technical Specifications:**
- Generator: 12V DC, 5W capacity
- Battery: 12V, 7Ah Lead-Acid
- Mechanism: Rack and Pinion with springs
- Expected Power: 8-10 Watts per vehicle crossing
- Estimated Daily Output: 50-60 Wh (100 vehicles)

**Outcomes & Learning:**
- Successfully demonstrated working prototype at college
- Generated power sufficient to light LED street lights
- Battery stored energy for continuous lighting
- Learned mechanical to electrical energy conversion principles
- Understood gear mechanisms and power transmission
- Gained practical fabrication and assembly experience
